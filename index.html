Try the full flow
  1. Open Client Booking, pick a car and dates, and request a booking.
  2. Open Admin Management in another tab and sign in with any email. The booking appears under Needs action.
  3. Back on Client Booking, open My bookings and enter a payment code. The admin sees it and can approve it.
