# Car photos

Run `bash download.sh` (Mac/Linux) or `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File download.ps1` (Windows) in this folder.
It saves 10 photos per car into four folders here. The site then uses these local files.
Until you do, the site loads the same photos straight from Wikimedia Commons.

To use your own photos, replace any `NN.jpg` with your picture (landscape, at least 1600 x 1000 px) and remove its row from the credits page.
